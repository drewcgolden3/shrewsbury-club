About the club

A family club, run by a family.

The Shrewsbury Club has been family owned and independently operated from the start, and it shows in how the place runs. Nate and Heather Sorensen built a club where a member can take a tennis clinic, drop the kids at camp, swim laps and lift, all without leaving the building.

You will find us just off Route 9 on the Shrewsbury and Northborough line, on a campus with tennis, pickleball, racquetball, batting cages, gymnastics, an outdoor pool and a full fitness floor.
